# Batch codes and dates

Triggers: batch code, batch, expiry, exp, made, manufacture, pao, 12m, open-jar, after opening, date

Batch questions are about dating, not identity: a batch code alone
never identifies a product. Verified made-date patterns cover Nivea,
P&G and L'Oreal groups; otherwise give printed-date guidance. Treat
medicine EXP as a safety date and best-before as a quality date; the
open-jar symbol (12M) counts from first opening.
