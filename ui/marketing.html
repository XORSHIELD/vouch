How It Works

Clarity without guesswork

Vouch separates evidence from conclusions. If something cannot be verified, it says so. Results are risk-based with plain explanations.

  1. Capture — image, pasted text, or barcode
  2. Extract — dates, batch, NAFDAC number
  3. Inspect — labels and available indicators
  4. Cross-check — NAFDAC register or product databases
  5. Report — findings with evidence and limitations
